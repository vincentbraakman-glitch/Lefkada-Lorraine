Where We Sail

The Ionian Islands — tucked along the western coast of Greece, just a short flight from major European cities.

Your 7-Day Journey

Each day unfolds with a balance of adventure, wellness, and deep connection to the beauty of the Greek islands.

Day 1 — Saturday

Settling Into the Ionian Rhythm

📍 Lefkada → Spartochori, Meganisi

The adventure begins in Lefkada, where you step aboard and ease into the calm rhythm of the sea. As the yacht glides toward Meganisi, the water shifts to deeper turquoise hues. You arrive in the charming marina of Spartochori, tucked beneath whitewashed houses and bougainvillea. To end the day, follow the 15-minute winding hillside path that leads from the harbor up into the heart of the village. It is a short, steady climb that rewards you with the island's most iconic sunset views across the Ionian. Dinner at a local taverna at 7 PM provides your first taste of authentic Greek island life.

Light stretchingSnorkeling
Day 2 — Sunday

Hidden Bays & Mediterranean Living

📍 Spartochori → Kioni, Ithaka

The morning begins with yoga on a nearby beach, followed by a sail toward the lush north of Ithaka. Slip into a secluded bay for snorkeling in crystal-clear water and swimming, then enjoy a nourishing onboard lunch inspired by the Greek blue zone diet. In the afternoon, enter Kioni, a classical Ionian harbor lined with tavernas. Join a local Greek cooking class where you'll learn traditional recipes and the stories behind them — and then sit down together to enjoy everything you've made. The workshop doubles as a full communal dinner, shared over wine as the sun dips into the sea.

Beach yogaCooking classSwimmingSnorkeling
Day 3 — Monday

Kefalonia by E-Bike & Cave

📍 Kioni → Sami, Kefalonia

A gentle morning sail carries you along Kefalonia's dramatic eastern coastline, where limestone cliffs meet clear Ionian waters. Pause in a secluded bay for snorkeling and paddleboarding before arriving in Sami. Set off on an e-bike tour through the lush countryside, culminating in the breathtaking Melissani Lake Cave — where turquoise waters glow beneath a dramatic sky opening.

Morning yogaE-bike tourMelissani CaveSnorkelingSwimming
Day 4 — Tuesday

Venetian Echoes

📍 Sami → Fiskardo, Kefalonia

The final stretch north brings you to Fiskardo, Kefalonia's most refined harbor, known for its Venetian architecture. Unwind with a full-body Ionian Signature massage — a deeply restorative treatment using local essential oils and sea salt, performed overlooking the sea. Beyond the spa, take a slow wander along the pine-lined coastal paths toward the old Venetian lighthouse, or explore the waterfront boutiques before the harbor comes alive with soft light for a memorable dinner ashore.

MeditationSpa visitSnorkeling
Day 5 — Wednesday

Northern Ithaka Magic & Ancient Paths

📍 Fiskardo → Frikes, Ithaka

The day begins with yoga on the beach before crossing back toward Ithaka's wild northeastern shores. After a refreshing swim and blue zone lunch onboard, we anchor in the quiet, mountain-framed harbor of Frikes. The afternoon is yours to ground yourself on land; you might follow the ancient path up to the village of Stavros to see the model of Odysseus's Palace and share a coffee with locals in the square, or simply take the gentle coastal trail toward Marmaka for a peaceful walk along the shoreline as the evening settles in.

Beach yogaSwimmingSnorkeling
Day 6 — Thursday

Deep Relaxation & Ridge Rituals

📍 Frikes → Sivota, Lefkada

The morning unfolds with calm sailing toward the green coves of Lefkada. Enjoy a midday breathwork session onboard to reset and reconnect. In the late afternoon, we arrive in the deep, protected bay of Sivota. As the golden hour approaches, head out for a contemplative walk along the Sivota Ridge. The trail winds through ancient olive groves on the hills surrounding the harbor, providing a spectacular bird's-eye view of the yacht fleet and the neighboring islands before we gather for our final evening in the village.

BreathworkSwimmingSnorkelingPaddle boarding
Day 7 — Friday

The Journey Home

📍 Sivota → Lefkada (Disembarkation)

Early morning yoga on the waterfront before the village awakens. After breakfast, the yacht makes a gentle return sail to Lefkada, offering one last moment to swim or snorkel in a quiet bay. As evening falls, gather for a final toast and sweet farewell lite meal — sharing stories and gratitude before returning home sun-warmed, restored, and deeply connected to the Ionian blue.
